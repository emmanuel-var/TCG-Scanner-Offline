# Privacy Policy – TCG Scanner Offline

_Last updated: 2026-10-02_

**Summary:** the app works on your device. We run no servers, collect no personal data, and show no ads.

## Data stored
Your collection, decks, portfolio history, settings and the downloaded card catalog are stored **only in the app's private storage on your device**. There is no account and nothing is uploaded to the developer.

## Network access
The app connects to public card-data services (such as Pokémon TCG API, Scryfall, MTGJSON, YGOPRODeck, OPTCG API, Lorcast, Lorcana API, DigimonCard.io, TCGdex, and – only if you enter your own credentials – TCGplayer and PriceCharting) to download catalogs, prices and card images. These services receive standard request data such as your IP address and, where you entered one, the API key for that service. Their own privacy policies apply.

## Camera
The camera feed is analysed in real time on the device (text recognition and image matching). Images are not stored or transmitted.

## Nearby devices (trading)
If you start a trade, the app uses Bluetooth / nearby Wi-Fi (and location permission on older Android versions, required by the system for device discovery) to connect to another phone you choose. After both users confirm a pairing code, your Trade Binder (card names, set, number, variant, quantity, value) is sent to that phone. Nothing is sent to any server.

## Export and deletion
You can export your data (CSV, JSON backup) at any time. “Erase all my data” in Settings or uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on the device.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
